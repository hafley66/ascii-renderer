## Anatomy notes observed

Pixel measurements come from #2, a neutral 3/4 standing cut-out 804 px tall. Treat them as approximate because the head is tilted forward.

- **Head-to-body ratio:** From skull top (y≈40) to chin (y≈120) is about 80 px. From skull top to soles (y≈790) is about 750 px. That makes him about **9 heads tall**, excluding the wheel. The head is small against the torso (#2, #1). #1 reads shorter because of the forward hunch and the low camera. Treat 8.5-9.5 heads as the working range.
- **Posture:** The head is carried low and forward of the chest. There is almost no visible neck because the traps rise straight into the skull (#4, #9, #2). In side view the ear line sits well in front of the shoulder line (#9).
- **Shoulders against hips:** At the deltoids the shoulders span about 295 px, against a head width of about 70 px (roughly 4 head widths in 3/4). At the sash the waist is about 150 px, so **shoulders are about 2x the waist/hip** (#2, #1, #4). The lats flare hard in the back view (#6).
- **Arm length:** The wrists (black rings) hang at about y≈400, near crotch level. Fingertips reach about y≈465, upper to mid thigh (#2, #1). The knees sit at about y≈640, so the fingertips stop well above them. The upper arm and forearm are about equal in length (#19).
- **Sword of Extermination:** A straight, thin, single-edged blade. It projects from the right hand along the axis of the bandaged right forearm (#1, #2, #8, #11). The bandage runs from the wrist to just below the elbow (#2, #19). In #2 the blade is about 215 px long, about 2.7 head lengths, and hangs to about hem/knee height. The left forearm is bare (#1, #2).
- **Wheel (Dharma wheel):** 8 spokes, with a sphere on each spoke end outside the rim, plus a central hub sphere (#10, #20, #5). Its diameter is about **2x the head width** (#2: ~150 px against ~70 px). It has two mountings:
  - A horizontal halo floating just above the crown in neutral or standing poses (#1, #2, #5).
  - Upright on the back or side of the head in action shots (#7, #14, #15, #17).
  - Make the mounting a rig option.
- **Eye appendages:** Two feathered, wing-like appendages grow out of each eye socket, four in total. They sweep up and outward and back, and cover the eyes completely (#4, #5, #10, #12). Each wing is roughly 1-1.5 head lengths long (#4, #5).
- **Face:** The lower face is a lipless grin of large square teeth with a heavy, bony jaw. The cranium above is long and ridged (#4, #5, #10, #12).
- **Head-tail:** A segmented, tapering tail-like extension grows from the back of the skull. It arcs back and down past the shoulder, with dark band marks between the segments (#2, #6, #15, #17). In #2 it is about 2+ head lengths long.
- **Necklace:** A chain of ring and hexagon links with short dark tassels. It lies across the clavicles about half a head below the chin, and dips at a central plate (#4, #10, #2, #1).
- **Torso:** Extreme definition, with an eight-pack, big pecs, serratus and oblique cuts (#4, #1, #18).
- **Skin:** Uniformly pale white to grey. There are no tattoo-like markings. The manga drawing uses hatch lines to show muscle (#4, #1, #11).
- **Clothing:**
  - A dark hakama-like wrap skirt, knee length or just below (#1, #2). The Modulo sketch draws it fuller, like bloomers, gathered to mid-calf (#9).
  - A dark waist band with a front plate (#2).
  - A light-grey sash knotted at the front, with a long tail hanging to about the hem (#1, #2, #8, #16).
- **Rings:** Black bands on both wrists and both ankles (#1, #2, #9).
- **Feet:** Bare, large and long-toed, with toes that splay and grip. They are wide-set in a planted stance (#1, #2, #8, #9).
- **Scale against a human:** Not measurable from these images because the depths are confounded. In #3 and #8 the figures are at different depths, so no height ratio is claimed.

## Rejected

- **Megumi summoning Mahoraga (anime, the wing cocoon):** It shows the summoning shell, not the body form.
- **Sukuna walking with Mahoraga far off (anime, "Sukuna after defeating Mahoraga"):** Mahoraga is a tiny silhouette.
- **Fire blast (anime, "Sukuna shooting flames at Mahoraga"):** The anatomy is not readable.
- **"Mahoraga shadow (Anime)":** It shows Megumi's hands, not Mahoraga.
- **"Mahoraga restrained by light beams" (manga):** A damaged, melting body that is off-model for the base form.
- **"Sukuna mounts Mahoraga" and "Mahoraga hits Sukuna across Shibuya" (manga):** Mahoraga is fragmentary or tiny in the panel.
- **Box photos from the figurestart.com Ichiban listing:** Packaging art. The official 1kuji shots (#1, #5, #13) are better.
- **ArtStation "Mahoraga Fan Model sheet" (Miodrag Vukcevic):** Fan art. It is useful as a turnaround idea but not canon.
- **An X/Twitter post claiming a MAPPA S2 Mahoraga settei sheet:** The post is not fetchable (HTTP 402), so the image could not be verified.
- **toy-people.com figure articles:** HTTP 403. The same figures were taken from the official 1kuji and segaplaza pages instead.
