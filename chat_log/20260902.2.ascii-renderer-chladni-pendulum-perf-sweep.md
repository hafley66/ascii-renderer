Hailing a parked lane with "restart the brief" recovered two partially; the rest was finished in-tree by the coordinator. Only perf-instr-modes-dir completed (merged `4aa6feb`).

Nine modes have one layer above 78 percent (cosmograph nebula, gem-aetherium background, tide sea, hyperloom spectral_field, glm-apotheosis background, aetherforge star_metal, qwen-cathedral background, tree-of-life-6 plane, sauron fire). Knobs move fps under 10 percent except braid-2 STRANDS, illuminarium RINGS, tide WAVES (~0.67x).
- fa6 renders natively but has no timers and is not in NATIVE_MODES. chimera, fireflies, ink, meteors, stained do not go through iterate_grid; sweep cannot time them.

## Solution/Approach
- Sweep = IterateFrameRenderer per knob at registry max via env ASCII_P_<KEY>, fps/p50/p99, worst knob rerun with in-process layer capture (opened after one warm-up frame).
- Instrumentation tool used in-tree: scratch `blocks.py` (list top-level blocks of a fn, wrap chosen ones by index with names); not committed.

## Key Insights
- Cost is resolution-bound, not knob-bound, for almost every mode; hotspot tables point at one full-grid pass each.
- `measure_layer` closures wrapping top-level `for` blocks are safe when the block has no `return`/`?`; `continue` inside is fine.
- Capture must open after the warm-up frame or shares exceed 100 percent.
- Lane briefs must carry CARGO_TARGET_DIR per lane; boop lane create has no env flag.
