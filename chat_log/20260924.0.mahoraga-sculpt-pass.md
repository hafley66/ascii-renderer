- Detail muscle tier (Shape.detail, DETAIL_BLEND 0.08 crease) for abs/serratus/obliques/back/toes/tail.
- Ink seams: per-vertex owner + SDF-Laplacian crease, chained + Laplacian-smoothed polylines (viewer only; not in skin.html).
- Props = triangle soups per bone (Prop{kind,bone,tris}): feathered wings, level gold dharma wheel, flat blade, wrist/ankle rings, forearm bandage, tail bands, necklace + knotted sash seated via onto_surface ray probes. Rendered in viewer and skin.html.
- Shape.bulge + sd_belly (parabolic mid-length swell), opt-in via .belly(fraction).
- BIND key (A-pose 32deg) split from K0 stand (arms hang 3deg, elbows 10-14, slack wrists) so skinning keeps no-web armpit.
- Proportions: ~9 heads, shoulders +-1.95 so arms hang straight down past lats, lats end higher (~1.7 below chest), head raised well above shoulder line (neck 1.3), traps 45deg cartoon-huge skull base->acromion, hakama transparent stand-in (cloth sim later), body/hakama surfaced separately.
