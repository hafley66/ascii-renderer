# Session: mahoraga-fire-walk-rig

## Goal
Build a Mahoraga (JJK S2E17 "thunderclap", Sukuna vs Mahoraga) ASCII art mode: white-hot figure with brown ink contours, salmon/rust/maroon watercolor fire washes, cream slash streaks. Mahoraga revives through flames and walks calmly and menacingly toward the camera while a domain expansion nudges him off line. Sukuna comes later. The step before the ASCII mode: a correct, tunable 3D rig and walk. The glam math and baked rig data will feed the Rust mode.

## Current State
- `examples/3_maha_rig.rs` (Rust + glam):
  - 34-joint rig with linked matrices (FK) and 5 keys K0-K4.
  - Body built from smooth-min round cones: core group blend 0.4, limb group 0.3, core↔limb 0.2. Trapezius is a 4-bundle fan; delt, delt-cap ball, pecs, lats, biceps/triceps/forearm bellies. The hakama is a capped cone.
  - Meshed with fast-surface-nets, voxel 0.2.
  - Subcommands: `skin` → docs/maha_rig/skin.html (the main viewer); `viewer` (older, remeshed per frame); `html` → rig.html (static wire/contour/ascii).
  - Bind pose is an A-pose (arms 32°).
  - Proportions follow the research (shoulders ±1.25, hips ±0.6, pelvis y 6.35); head scaled ×0.7.
  - Blend shapes (morph deltas projected onto the new SDF zero set): traps height/mass/reach/slope; delt mass/length/out/cap.
- `examples/maha_skin.html` (three.js template, `__RIG__` injected):
  - SkinnedMesh with 4 bone weights per vertex, morph targets.
  - Views: wire (bloom), contour (inverted-hull outline), live ASCII.
  - Sidebar: foldable, collapsible groups, instant tooltips. Hover a knob + S to sweep it; click pins, S again or mouse-out restores.
  - Knob state in the URL hash; presets saved to localStorage.
  - Walk and body presets are separate sections and only touch their own knobs. Walk presets are the user's six: taking the domain, drunk on cursed energy, wading through fire, shoulder-led, hip-led swagger, after the adaptation.
  - Legs: analytic 2-bone IK with world-pinned stance feet, stance knee profile, ball-of-foot peel, heel strike, early lift. Hip height is solved from the planted legs (ridingHipY).
  - Upper body: shoulder-led sway, arm bias.
  - Randomness: seeded step/foot jitter, arm variation, drift, head look.
  - Domain pressure: pushX buffet with bracing.
  - Head level and head line are IK goals over spine/chest/neck/head (CCD lean plus a distributed orientation split).
  - Camera-angle knob.
- Research docs: docs/maha_rig/research_gait.md (gait and proportion targets, 8 IK bugs, all applied) and research_libs.md (MakeHuman/Anny CC0 body, 100STYLE/CMU heavyset walk, gltf crate).
- Everything is committed on main in ascii-renderer; the last commit is d8571ba. The user wants everything to stay in this repo.

## Key Insights
- The wireframe of primitives lied: build it from the blended surface (surface nets).
- Morph deltas via gradient projection keep the topology fixed, so blend shapes can be live sliders.
- Constant leg "reach" = constant knee crouch; use the knee profile instead.
- A negative first-frame dt made phase negative and folded the legs; clamp dt and wrap the phase.
- Headless Chrome (swiftshader) screenshots and `--dump-dom` with a debug attribute are the verification path.
- The comment-prod hook rejects more than 2 consecutive comment lines; CSS `#id` lines count as comments, so use `div#id`.
- A subagent via Agent model "opus" = Opus 5.5.
